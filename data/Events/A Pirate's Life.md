**Note:** The route encounters only appear on routes that have water Pokémon, and only on a random half of them; which routes those are is decided by the year, so it changes every event. Alola's Magikarp Jump sub-region is always excluded.

- Only [[Pokémon/Gyarados (Captain)]] is available as a Dungeon Boss from the start. [[Pokémon/Tsareena (Captain)]] and [[Pokémon/Cinderace (Captain)]] only appear as bosses once you already own them, so they have to be evolved first. [[Pokémon/Captain Zacian]] requires reaching Galar and clearing [[Dungeons/Pirate Island]] 100 times.
- The pirate Pokémon evolve into their Captain forms: [[Pokémon/Magikarp (Pirate)]] into [[Pokémon/Gyarados (Captain)]] at level 20, [[Pokémon/Bounsweet (Pirate)]] into [[Pokémon/Steenee (Pirate)]] at level 18 and then [[Pokémon/Tsareena (Captain)]] at level 29, and [[Pokémon/Scorbunny (Pirate)]] into [[Pokémon/Raboot (Pirate)]] at level 16 and then [[Pokémon/Cinderace (Captain)]] at level 35.
- [[Pokémon/Zorua (Pirate)]] is bought from the Shady Fence trader for 100 Pirate Coins, and only becomes visible once you have reached Unova.
- Beating the [[Temporary Battles/Gyarados Crew]] battle in [[Towns/Gyarados Galleon]] awards a treasure map, starting a quest to clear a dungeon in your highest reached region, completing it awards Pirate Coins. The battle resets daily.

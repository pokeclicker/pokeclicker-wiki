The Veteran Shop is a shop in [[Towns/Pallet Town]] exclusive to experienced players. It allows certain mechanics to be unlocked early, with each offering requiring a specific unlock condition to have been met on a **separate save file**. The shop only appears if at least one mechanic or item is available for purchase.

###### New Locations & Features
- [[Routes/Trophy Garden]] added to Sinnoh, unlocked after 10 defeats on [[Routes/Sinnoh Route 212]]. Alongside its 5 regular encounters, Mr. Backlot brings in 3 different Pokémon each day from a list of 33 - but only ones you have already caught elsewhere. He can be found in [[Towns/Hearthome City]]
- [[Veteran Shop]] added to [[Towns/Pallet Town]], letting experienced players unlock certain mechanics early based on progress made on a **separate save file**
- New _Events_ [[Achievements]] category, with catch/shiny/Pokérus achievements for each event's Pokémon and clear achievements for [[Dungeons/New Island]]
- A **Perfumer** in [[Towns/Agate Village]] trades 12 [[Items/Joy Scent]] for an [[Items/Excite Scent]], and 12 Excite Scents for a [[Items/Vivid Scent]]
- New NPCs give hints toward easily missed locations - the [[Towns/Route 4 Pokémon Center]] (hinted in [[Towns/Pewter City]]), the [[Towns/Wind Chime Shop]] (Fortree City), the [[Towns/Secret Berry Shop]] (Snowpoint City) and the [[Towns/Disguised Shop]] (Geosenge Town)

###### Pokémon Location/Evolution Changes
- [[Pokemon/Castform]] now changes into [[Pokemon/Castform (Sunny)]], [[Pokemon/Castform (Rainy)]] or [[Pokemon/Castform (Snowy)]] depending on the current [[Weather]]
- [[Pokemon/Cosmoem]] now evolves into [[Pokemon/Solgaleo]] during the day and [[Pokemon/Lunala]] at night
- [[Pokemon/Kyurem]] is now always available in [[Dungeons/Giant Chasm]], regardless of your progress in the _Swords of Justice_ questline
- [[Pokemon/Feebas]] can now be found on [[Routes/Hoenn Route 119]] after being obtained
- [[Pokemon/Pachirisu]] can now be found on [[Routes/Sinnoh Route 205]] after being obtained
- [[Pokemon/Drampa]] can now be found in [[Dungeons/Mina's Houseboat]] after being obtained
- [[Pokemon/Yanmega]] and [[Pokemon/Masquerain]] are now water encounters instead of grass encounters in the [[Safari]]
- [[Pokemon/Cradily]] removed from [[Dungeons/Max Lair]]
- During _The Eon Duo_ questline, [[Pokemon/Latios]] and [[Pokemon/Latias]] only roam once you have obtained the other one. Both roam normally after the questline is finished
- Event Pokémon (Clone, Spooky, Rose and Elf forms) can now only evolve while their event is running
- Encounter lists cleaned up to better match the original games: [[Pokemon/Zubat]] removed from [[Routes/Johto Route 30]] and [[Routes/Johto Route 31]], [[Pokemon/Poliwag]] moved to the water on Johto Route 31, [[Pokemon/Gastrodon (East)]] removed from [[Routes/Sinnoh Route 222]] and [[Pokemon/Chatot]] removed from [[Routes/Sinnoh Route 224]]

###### [[Farm]] Changes
- [[Berries/Chople]], [[Berries/Kebia]], [[Berries/Shuca]] and [[Berries/Charti]] no longer need an [[Oak Items]] to mutate. Instead, grow their parent berry ([[Berries/Spelon]], [[Berries/Pamtre]], [[Berries/Watmel]] or [[Berries/Cornn]]) next to exactly three of its own kind with nothing else nearby. Having the matching Oak Item equipped doubles the chance. These four berries are also no longer sold by the [[Berry Masters]] or the Cerulean City Exclusive Berry Shop
- The legendary berries ([[Berries/Liechi]], [[Berries/Ganlon]], [[Berries/Salac]], [[Berries/Apicot]] and [[Berries/Lansat]]) now unlock by completing the _Land vs. Water_ or _A New World_ questline instead of catching the matching legendary Pokémon. Catching that legendary now doubles the mutation chance instead, and hints have been added for each
- [[Berries/Rindo]] now has an aura that increases the chance for wild Pokémon to drop [[Rare Hold Items]]
- [[Berries/Jaboca]] grows much faster and its roaming aura was buffed from 0.5/1/1.5% to 1/2/3%
- [[Pokemon/Snover]] can now drop [[Berries/Snover]] Berries, at the rate the Snover mutation would occur on your farm
- Berry plants now flash when they are close to withering, and the warning comes 5 minutes ahead instead of 15 seconds
- Catching a [[Wandering Pokémon]] now lowers berry shop prices, the same as harvesting
- Wandering Pokémon now count toward your _Encountered_ statistics
- Gooey Mulch weighting has been increased from 2 → 3

###### [[Safari]] Changes
- The Razz and Nanab bait effects have been swapped - Razz now increases the catch rate, Nanab lowers the flee rate
- New **Pinap** bait, which gives a second chance for an item to appear when you catch a Pokémon
- Added a setting for a female player sprite
- Added tooltips explaining what each bait does

###### [[Dungeons]] Changes
- Entry cost now goes down as the dungeon shrinks from repeated clears
- Dungeon loot info now shows the chance of obtaining each item
- Mimics now appear in the loot table
- [[Dungeon Guides]] play a sound when they finish all their attempts

###### Item & Shop Changes
- Master Balls no longer stop getting more expensive at 100× the base price - the price now rises indefinitely
- [[Items/Power Herb]] now boosts Attack by 40% in the [[Battle Frontier]] instead of 50% against dungeon bosses. It can now be bought in the Battle Frontier shop for 2,500 [[Battle Points]], and drops in [[Dungeons/Santalune Forest]] and [[Dungeons/Flower Paradise]]
- The [[Oak Items/Cell Battery]] now increases how much the [[Underground]] battery charges per mining attempt (up to 2×) instead of lowering the charges needed. The battery display now shows a percentage
- The Time Flute now also increases Flow gained in the Purify Chamber
- The Poké Mart shortcut now has a Farm tab with Mulch and Shovels, available once you have 25 farm plots and have beaten Champion Cynthia
- [[Berries/Pinkan]] Berries can now be found as loot in [[Dungeons/Pinkan Mountain]]
- Halloween Pokémon can now drop [[Items/Rare Candy]]
- The Scent held items now correctly state that they work on Shadow **and** Purified Pokémon

###### Other Changes
- [[Battle Café]] spins now build up over time (up to 7 days' worth) instead of resetting each day, and a spin is not used up if the resulting Alcremie is already resistant to Pokérus
- Battle Frontier Pokémon levels are no longer capped at stage 100, icnreasing the EXP gained
- [[Items/Medichamite]] is now twice as likely to drop at higher Quest levels
- Improved the formula for [[Contest Tokens]] earned in the Bug Catching Contest, so weaker Pokémon are worth more
- Some [[Environments]] were updated to better match their location
- Kanto and Johto rival [[Temporary Battles]] now unlock in a more sensible order, and the Kalos rival battles return you to the correct town

###### UI & Quality of Life
- Added sorting to the Pokédex
- Several [[Hatchery]] and Pokédex filters now let you select multiple options at once, and there are new _Reset All Filters_ buttons
- [[Hatchery Helpers]] now show which Pokémon they will hatch next
- Route info now shows your chance of finding a [[Roaming Pokémon]]
- Held item filters gained options to filter by item quality
- Berry lists now have tooltips showing berry info
- Pokémon stats can now be opened from the dungeon encounter view
- Farm auras now indicate whether they are positive or negative
- Added a backup button to the next region travel modal
- Clicking the save icon now shows a notification
- Route and dungeon shortcut keys now work while the Dungeon info menu is open
- Challenge descriptions can now be read directly, and lots of smaller UI and text improvements

###### Bug Fixes
- [[Berries/Lum]] Berry no longer incorrectly cancels out the [[Berries/Micle]] Berry
- [[Dungeon Guides]] no longer get stuck if you move manually
- Fixed downloading and copying save data on iOS devices
- Fixed the [[Oak Items/Magic Ball]] not gaining XP from Safari captures
- Fixed the shiny [[Pokemon/Valencian Raticate]] sprite
- Fixed various styling issues across several themes
